Of the workshop

Three works of one hand

WWithin these leaves are set down the three works of this workshop, made at Brazil since the year MMXXIV. Two are finished and went out into the world. The third is still upon the bench.

This workshop makes systems-driven games: worlds whose parts are built to act upon one another, so that what happens next was not written down beforehand. Every hour now goes into Dead Saints Parade, a third-person stealth immersive sim for PC and Steam Deck, developed and published in-house. Before it came two idle titles on Google Play, Idle Kingdom Builder and Idle Cyberpunk City Clicker, which have 1,600+ organic downloads between them.

The work unfinished · the third

III

Dead Saints Parade

Third-person stealth immersive sim · PC & Steam Deck · Unity 6

Quod scis plus valet quam quod portas.What you know is worth more than what you carry

You are set down in a decaying Gothic-Victorian city as a fragile spy. The blade kills you as readily as it kills them, so the knife is a poor instrument and what you know is a better one. The guards speak to one another, light and sound will give you up, and the common people repeat what they have heard, so anything overheard can be turned and used against its owner.

The tension comes from those parts reacting to each other rather than from scripted moments. Made after the manner of Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid and Gloomwood.

The works completed · the first and the second

Printed and sent out

Both were made alone, both went out on Google Play, and neither was given a coin of marketing. Between them, 1,600+ downloads. The first went out blind, with no instruments at all and no honest way to see where a player stopped. The second carried instruments at four milestones, and those readings drove five corrections.

I

Idle Kingdom Builder

Idle clicker · Android · Unity 2022

Quod primum est, docet.What comes first, teaches

The first thing out the door. The loop and its economy were drawn up from nothing, with Cookie Clicker's formulas held against them as a measure, then tuned slower and more deliberate: ten buildings from House to Bank, an upgrade path on each, and a Royal Ascension that resets the whole and begins it again. Nothing in it was instrumented, so there was never a way to see where players stopped. That gap is what the second work was built to close.

II

Idle Cyberpunk City Clicker

Idle clicker · Android · Unity 6

Quod non mensuratur non emendatur.What is not measured is not mended

The second builds on what the first taught: deeper progression tiers, with a layer of rewarded ads and purchases laid over them. Unity Analytics takes a reading of the player's state at four milestones, and that is how two faults in the economy and one in the timer's persistence were found. Five corrections went out across v1.70 and v1.81, and a second reading set the economy against the frozen baseline from before the overhaul.
