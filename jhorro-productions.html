Dead Saints Parade

Third-Person Stealth Immersive Sim · PC & Steam Deck · Unity 6

You are a fragile spy inside a decaying Gothic-Victorian world where combat is lethal in both directions and information is the real weapon. Guards coordinate, light and sound betray you, civilians talk, and everything you observe becomes leverage. Stealth tension engineered through systems rather than scripting — inspired by Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid, and Gloomwood.
