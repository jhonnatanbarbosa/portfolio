Dead Saints Parade
Third-Person Stealth Immersive Sim · PC & Steam Deck · Unity 6

You are a fragile spy inside a decaying Gothic-Victorian world where combat is lethal in both directions and information is the real weapon. Guards coordinate, light and sound betray you, civilians talk, and everything you observe becomes leverage. The tension comes from those systems reacting to each other rather than from scripted moments. It takes after Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid, and Gloomwood.

Two idle titles shipped solo on Google Play before the studio turned to PC. Between them they have 1,600+ organic downloads, with no marketing spend behind either. The first went out with no analytics at all. The second was instrumented at four progression milestones, and those snapshots drove five balance fixes.

Idle Cyberpunk City Clicker
Idle Clicker · Android · Unity 6

Built on what the first game taught: deeper progression tiers and a monetization layer of rewarded ads and IAP. Unity Analytics snapshots player state at four milestones, which is how two economy bugs and a timer persistence defect surfaced. Five fixes went out across v1.70 and v1.81, then the economy was measured again against the frozen pre-overhaul baseline.

Idle Kingdom Builder
Idle Clicker · Android · Unity 2022

The first game out the door. Core loop and economy designed from scratch with Cookie Clicker's formulas as the reference, then tuned toward a slower, more deliberate pace: ten building types from House to Bank, an upgrade path on each, and a Royal Ascension prestige reset. It shipped with no analytics at all, so there was no reliable way to see where players stopped. That gap is what Cyberpunk City was instrumented to close.
