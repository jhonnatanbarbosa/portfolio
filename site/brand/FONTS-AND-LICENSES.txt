JHORRO PRODUCTIONS - FONTS AND LICENSES
=======================================

Short answer: all three brand fonts are free for commercial use, including in
the logo, in the games, on the website, on merch and in print. They are all
released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL), as stated inside
the font files themselves.

The font files are NOT included in this kit. Download them from Google Fonts:

  Archivo         https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Archivo
  Michroma        https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Michroma
  IBM Plex Mono   https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

The logo files in this kit are drawn as outlines, so opening or using them
needs no fonts at all.
