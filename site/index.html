Dead Saints Parade
Third-Person Stealth Immersive Sim · PC & Steam Deck · Unity 6

You play a spy in a decaying Gothic-Victorian city. Information is your best weapon, and a fight kills you about as fast as it kills a guard. Guards work together, and light and sound give you away. Civilians repeat what they hear. The game takes after Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid and Gloomwood.

The core systems are done and the demo is 62% complete. The cathedral levels, dagger and pistol combat, and Steam Deck controls are being built now.

Dead Saints Parade: how it playsDetails

Four ideas run through Dead Saints Parade: vulnerability, tactical stealth, information as power, and non-linear exploration. Cover and guard vision are two of the systems already working in the build.

Cover

There is no cover button. Walk into a wall and you take cover, then push away from it to leave. In cover you move slower and can't sprint.

At a corner, the camera looks around it while your body stays where it is. You can't slide around the corner onto the next wall. To change walls you step out and take cover again, and for that moment you are exposed.

Guard vision

A guard has two fields of view. The narrow one straight ahead reaches 15 m and sees you whether you move or not. The wide one reaches 8 m and only picks up movement, and it does that slowly. If a guard turns your way while you are at the edge of their vision, standing still keeps you hidden.

Light matters as well. In deep shadow a guard can't see you at any distance, so putting out a single candle can be worth the time.
