Jhorro Productions is an independent game studio in Brazil. The current project is Dead Saints Parade, a third-person stealth game for PC and Steam Deck. Two idle games are out on Google Play: Idle Kingdom Builder and Idle Cyberpunk City Clicker.

Dead Saints Parade
Third-Person Stealth Immersive Sim | PC & Steam Deck | Unity 6

You play a spy in a decaying Gothic-Victorian city. Information is your best weapon, and a fight kills you about as fast as it kills a guard. Guards work together, and light and sound give you away. Civilians repeat what they hear. The game takes after Thief, Dishonored, Metal Gear Solid and Gloomwood.

The core systems are done and the demo is 62% complete. The cathedral levels, dagger and pistol combat, and Steam Deck controls are being built now.

GenreThird-person stealth immersive sim, single-player

AboutJhorro Productions

Jhorro Productions is an independent game studio in São Paulo, Brazil, founded by game designer Jhonnatan Barbosa. It makes stealth and immersive-sim games for PC and idle games for mobile, and does its own design and programming.

The name has been in use since 2022. The first game came out in 2024, and the studio was incorporated in 2026.
