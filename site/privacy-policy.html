Privacy PolicyLast updated: July 25th, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected by the Android games published by Jhorro Productions ("we," "us," "our") — the independent studio run by Jhonnatan Barbosa. It currently covers Idle Kingdom Builder and Idle Cyberpunk City Clicker, and applies to any future Android game we publish unless that game links to a different policy.

Short version — we don't collect your name, email, or anything else that identifies you directly. Some games use anonymous gameplay analytics (with your consent, where the law requires it) and ad networks that rely on your device's advertising ID. Full details below.

Information We Do Not Collect01

None of our games require an account, and we never ask for personally identifying information like your name, email address, or phone number. You can download and play without handing us anything personal directly.

Anonymous Gameplay Analytics02

Some of our games use Unity Analytics, part of Unity Gaming Services, to see how players progress and to catch bugs early. This can include gameplay events — like milestones reached, items or buildings unlocked, and time spent — plus general device information such as device model, OS version, and country. This data is aggregated and isn't linked to your name or any contact details in our systems.

Where the law requires it (for example, players in the EU/EEA or UK), the game shows a consent screen before this collection starts. You're free to decline, and the game works exactly the same either way. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for how Unity itself handles this data.

Advertising03

To help keep our games free, some of them show ads through networks like Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These networks can use your device's advertising ID to choose and measure ads, and — with your consent, where required — to personalize them. You can opt out of ad personalization anytime in your device settings, or decline personalized ads when asked in-game; you'll still see ads, just less targeted ones. Reward ads (the kind that give an in-game bonus) are always opt-in — none play without you choosing to start one.

See Google's page on how it uses information from apps that use its services for more.

In-App Purchases04

A few of our games offer optional in-app purchases. These are processed entirely by Google Play's billing system — we never see or store your card details, only what you bought.

Data Collected by Google Play05

Like any app on the Play Store, our games automatically send Google basic crash reports and device performance data, which helps us find and fix bugs. This information doesn't identify you personally.

Children's Privacy06

Our games are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we've inadvertently received such information, we will delete it from our records promptly.

Your Choices07

You can reset or opt out of your device's advertising ID anytime in your Android settings, and withdrawing analytics consent or uninstalling a game stops any further collection tied to that install. If you'd like to ask what data, if any, is associated with your device, or want it deleted, just email us below and we'll help.

Changes to This Policy08

We may update this policy as our games, or the services they rely on, change. Updates will be posted right here, with a new "last updated" date at the top.

Contact Us09

Questions about this policy? Reach us at support@jhorro.com.
